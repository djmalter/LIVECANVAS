# Privacy Policy Template for Live Canvas Studio

*Last updated: September 2026*

LiveCanvas Studio ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed with **Privacy by Design** principles. This privacy policy describes how your information is handled when using LiveCanvas Studio.

## 1. Information Collection & Usage

- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register an account or provide personal identifying details.
- **Zero Telemetry by Default:** We do not include third-party analytics trackers, crash beacons, or hidden diagnostic logging.
- **Camera & Microphone:** Accessed exclusively during active, user-initiated scene previewing, recording, or live streaming. Audio and video frames remain local to your device unless you initiate an outbound RTMP stream.
- **Media Files:** The app utilizes the standard Android Photo Picker and System File Picker. We never perform broad background storage scans.
- **Screen Projection:** When screen sharing is enabled, Android displays its standard system consent dialog. Screen frames are processed locally inside the studio.
- **Stream Keys & Credentials:** Stored locally on your device using Android encrypted preferences. Stream keys are never uploaded to any intermediary server.

## 2. Platform Compliance Statement

LiveCanvas Studio functions strictly within its own recording and streaming workflow. It does not replace the Android system camera or inject video feeds into third-party communication applications.

## 3. Data Deletion

You can clear all stored scenes, recent media references, and studio preferences at any time by going to **Settings > Delete All App Data & Scenes**.
